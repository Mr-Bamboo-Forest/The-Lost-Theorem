How to Play
THE LOST THEOREM — A MATHEMATICS RPG
The Story
Ten mathematical worlds have been sealed by a lost theorem. Explore each world, seek out its scholars and keepers, and solve their problems to recover the Artefacts that will restore what was lost.
Controls
WASD / ARROWS
Move one tile per keypress. Hold to move continuously.
SPACE / ENTER
Interact with a nearby character.
E
Open your Inventory.
ESC
Return to the World Map.
⚙ (cog)
Settings: fullscreen, volume, progress reset, and this guide.
Terrain
Most tiles are walkable. Blocked tiles — water, mountains, trees, and ruin walls — have a subtle amber glow at their edges. You cannot walk through them. Characters stand on walkable tiles; walk adjacent to one and press SPACE to talk.
Encounters
Each character gives you a maths problem drawn from their world's topic. You have 3 attempts. A correct answer earns an Artefact. After each encounter, characters enter a cooldown period before they can be challenged again. Faded characters are on cooldown.
Mobile
A virtual joystick appears automatically on touch devices. Use the TALK button to interact. The INVENTORY button opens your collected Artefacts. The ⚙ settings cog in-game gives access to the World Map, Inventory, fullscreen, and volume.
