How to Play
THE LOST THEOREM
The World
One massive world divided into mathematical zones. You start in the zone at the centre. Every 3 artefacts you collect unlocks a new zone, removing a barrier wall and expanding the map. Harder topics are further from the centre.
Zombies
Zombies spawn from purple spawners labelled as [SP] and are scattered around the map. They slowly pathfind toward you. You have 5 lives. Touching a zombie kills it but costs a life. You have a brief invincibility window after each hit. When you die (0 lives) you respawn at the start and lose all artefacts.
Artefacts and Bonuses
Each NPC gives an artefact for a correct answer. Some artefacts grant an extra heart and some grant a timed shield that makes zombies wither on contact without costing a life. The type is random each run.
