About The Lost Theorem

A free maths adventure game built for students

What Is The Lost Theorem?

The Lost Theorem is a free browser-based mathematics RPG set across ten unique worlds, each themed around a different area of mathematics. Players explore hand-crafted tile maps, encounter characters with their own backstories, and answer curriculum-aligned maths problems to earn Artefacts and unlock the lore of each world.

The game covers topics from integration and differentiation to complex numbers, probability, statistics, vectors, and combinatorics, making it a practical study companion as much as a game.

Who Made It?

The Lost Theorem is an independent passion project developed by a Queensland high school student who has a love for mathematics, game design, and making hard concepts feel accessible. The game was built entirely in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, meaning there is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for. It runs directly in your browser.

The project is open-source and hosted on GitHub. Development is ongoing, with new worlds, NPCs, and problem sets being added regularly.

The Worlds

Ten mathematical worlds are available to explore:

  • The Integral Plains — Calculus: integration and differentiation
  • Euclid Eutopia — Geometry: circles, triangles, vectors
  • Probability Hollow — Probability: distributions, expected value, Bayes
  • Algebra Aces — Algebra: polynomials, sequences, logarithms, induction
  • Trig Peaks — Trigonometry: identities, equations, the unit circle
  • Derivation City — Calculus: derivatives, chain rule, related rates
  • The Vector Wastelands — Vectors: dot product, cross product, 3D geometry
  • Exponential Depths — Exponentials and logarithms
  • The Combinatorial Archives — Combinatorics: permutations, combinations, derangements
  • The Null Space — Complex numbers: modulus, argument, De Moivre, loci
