May 2026  •  Update v1.0
v1.0: One World, Eleven Zones, and Zombie Survival

The biggest structural change in the game's history. Eleven separate worlds have been merged into one massive open map — 160x120 tiles — divided into zones that unlock as you collect Artefacts. Algebra is the starting zone at the centre. Every other zone radiates outward, harder topics further from the start.

Zombie survival. Zombies now spawn from purple skull spawners placed throughout each zone. They path slowly but persistently toward you using a BFS-lite algorithm with shuffled tie-breaking so movement feels organic. You have five lives. Touching a zombie costs a life and converts it to a grave tile. Questions freeze all zombies mid-step. Shields and extra lives can drop from Artefact rewards.

Zone unlock arrows. When a zone unlocks, a glowing directional arrow orbits the screen for 15 seconds pointing toward the newly accessible area. It fades in, pulses, and fades out, and shows the zone name and distance in tiles. It disappears automatically once you walk close enough to see the zone.

Progress bar HUD. A colour-matched progress bar in the top-left shows how many Artefacts remain until the next zone unlock. The bar colour updates as you move between zones.

AdSense integration. Placeholder ad slots are now in place across all content pages ready for publisher ID and slot ID replacement once approved.

April 2026  •  Update v0.9
v0.9: 77 NPCs, Question Bank Overhaul, Tap-to-Talk

The question bank grew from 33 NPCs to 77 in this update. Every zone now has between 5 and 9 NPCs, each with three questions drawn from their specific topic. New NPCs include the Quadratic Monk, Substitution Witch, Integration by Parts Bishop, Volume Sculptor, Binomial Wizard, Normal Nurse, Poisson Hermit, Double Angle Sage, Quotient Alchemist, Tangent Painter, Pascal's Keeper, Roots of Unity Sage, Locus Painter, Regression Oracle, and Central Limit Guardian, among many others.

Tap-to-talk. Tapping or clicking on the canvas while adjacent to an NPC now starts dialogue, in addition to the SPACE/ENTER keyboard shortcut and the TALK joystick button on mobile.

Bug fixes. A missing opening bracket on the Inclusion Sage's dialogue array caused a syntax error that silently broke all NPCs defined after it in the file. Fixed. A null guard was also added to openProb so NPCs without question bank entries exit cleanly rather than hanging.

March 2026  •  Update v0.8
v0.8: Settings Overhaul, IAB Redirect, Music System

Three standalone HUD buttons replaced with a single cog settings panel. From the panel: World Map, Inventory, Fullscreen toggle, Volume slider, Blocked Tile Glow toggle, Joystick toggle, How To Play, and Reset All Progress.

IAB redirect. Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and other in-app browsers intercept links and open them in a sandboxed browser that breaks the game. A redirect snippet at the top of the page now detects these browsers and sends users to their real browser with an instruction screen as fallback.

Music system. Placeholder file paths for per-zone audio tracks were added. Tracks shuffle randomly from each zone's pool on entry and loop, picking a new random track when the current one ends.

Critical bug fixes. Object.defineProperty on gs.screen was removed — it caused a re-entrant setter that left overlay elements blocking input after reset or fullscreen cycling. pointer-events:none added to all display:none overlays so invisible elements can never swallow clicks.

February 2026  •  Update v0.7
v0.7: Virtual Joystick, Movement Fix, Blocked Tile Glow

A touch-based virtual joystick with dead zone and clamped travel appears automatically on mobile. A single keypress now moves exactly one tile with immediate response; holding continues movement after a delay; releasing stops cleanly. Impassable tiles can optionally show a subtle amber glow, toggled in settings and off by default.

January 2026  •  Update v0.6
v0.6: Inventory, Progress Persistence, Cooldown System

The Inventory screen shows all collected Artefacts in a grid with zone attribution. Progress now persists across sessions using localStorage. NPC cooldowns survive browser refreshes. The Reset Progress option in settings wipes both artefacts and cooldowns. NPCs on cooldown render faded with no interaction marker.

December 2025  •  v0.1 — v0.5
Origins: From a Single File to a Working Game

The Lost Theorem started as a single question: can a meaningful maths game fit in one HTML file? v0.1 was a canvas renderer and a player character that could walk. v0.2 added NPC proximity detection and the first dialogue boxes. v0.3 introduced the tile engine, the palette system, and the first three worlds. v0.4 brought the full dialogue and problem system with three attempts per NPC. v0.5 added The Null Space and the first version of the Inventory.

The decision to cover eleven mathematical topics came from mapping the Queensland senior mathematics curriculum and noticing that each major topic had enough depth to sustain its own set of characters and environments.
